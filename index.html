How it works

  1. 1
    Select text on any page
    Right-click and choose "Fact-check selection."
  2. 2
    Claims are narrowed down for you
    Up to three specific, checkable claims are pulled out of the selection and shown to you — editable — before anything else happens.
  3. 3
    Real sources get searched
    A live search runs for each claim against real search results — no static knowledge, no guessing.
  4. 4
    See the verdict and the evidence
    Each source is weighed by your own trust rating and whether it actually supports or refutes the claim, combined into one verdict and confidence score — with every source listed right there.

Built to be inspected, not just trusted

The model never answers from memory
Every verdict is grounded in retrieved evidence you can see — the model is only ever asked to judge whether a specific snippet supports or refutes a claim, never to recall the answer itself.
You decide who to trust
Rate domains on your own scale — a source's weight in the verdict comes from your trust list, not a fixed opinion baked into the extension.
The verdict's color tells you the verdict
Green means true, orange means false or unsupported — a separate confidence bar shows how strong the evidence was, so color and confidence never get conflated.

Purpose of the site

accordingto.app is the website of according to, a browser extension that checks claims on a web page against sources found by a live web search, and reports what those sources say. The site describes the extension, hosts its account area, and sells access to it.

Consumer information

Subscriptions are billed by Stripe, and can be cancelled at any time from Stripe's billing portal, reachable from the extension's Account tab or from the pricing section of this site. For questions or complaints, write to support@accordingto.app.

Liability for links

This site and the extension both link to third-party pages whose content is outside our control. The extension in particular surfaces search results: a source appearing in a result is a statement about what that source says, never an endorsement of it, and responsibility for its content rests with its own publisher.
